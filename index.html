Featured research

Is Chronos-2 honest about its uncertainty?

Amazon's Chronos-2 is a time series foundation model. I audited its prediction intervals on Sensex and Nifty. Its 80% intervals covered only 71–75% of real outcomes beyond day one.

Coverage also shifts with the volatility regime, and the pattern flips with the forecast horizon. A conformal correction narrowed the gap, from 75.2% to 78.7% at 16 days, but did not fully close it. That last part matters: an honest result beats a flattering one.
